# History

Records of how Extend was built, verified and released before Extend 4 moved it to Silicon
Accounts and Silicon Apps. They describe Silicon IAM, Honeycomb, organizations and test
environments as they were then, and they are kept as they were written: read them for why
something is the way it is, never for how to do it now. The current guides are in
[`docs/`](../) and the [README](../../README.md); the move itself is recorded in
[`docs/migration/`](../migration/).

| [completion-work.md](completion-work.md) | The open gates and Carbon decisions for 1.1.0 (2026-09-28) |
| [deployment-1.x-3.x.md](deployment-1.x-3.x.md) | Release and rollback procedures for 1.1.0 to 3.1.1, including Rolling back to 1.0.0 |
